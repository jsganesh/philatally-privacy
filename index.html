Philatally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Philatally is an iPhone app for stamp collectors. It helps you photograph, catalogue, and learn about your stamp collection. This policy explains what information Philatally handles. In short: your data stays on your device. Philatally collects no data about you.

Data we do not collect

Philatally does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Your stamp collection, photos, and notes are stored only on your device.

Camera and Photo Library

Philatally uses your device camera to photograph stamps and your photo library to import existing stamp images. These images are stored only on your device as part of your collection. They are never uploaded anywhere. You are only asked for permission when you choose to add a stamp.

Stamp lookups (Search History)

If you choose to look up a stamp online, Philatally sends your search text directly to the public Wikidata/Wikipedia services to return reference information. This is always initiated by you, one stamp at a time. The search text is not saved, is not linked to your identity, and is used only to provide the reference result you requested.

In-app purchase

Philatally is free for up to 100 stamps and offers one optional in-app purchase that removes the limit. The purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Philatally never sees your payment details; it only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the purchase has been made, and remembers the answer on your device. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your data, your control

  • Backup: you can export your collection to a backup file and import it again on this device.
  • Export: you can export your collection as a CSV file.
  • Deletion: you can delete any stamp or note in the app. Deleting the app from your device removes all remaining Philatally data.

Children

Philatally is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
