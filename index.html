PhilaTally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

PhilaTally is an iPhone and iPad app for stamp collectors. It helps you photograph, catalogue, and learn about your stamp collection. This policy explains what information PhilaTally handles. In short: your data stays on your device unless you send or copy it somewhere yourself. PhilaTally collects no data about you.

Data we do not collect

PhilaTally does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Your stamp collection, photos, and notes are stored on your device. They leave it only in the ways described under "Copies you make" below, and each of those is something you start or switch on.

Camera and Photo Library

PhilaTally uses your device camera to photograph stamps and your photo library to import existing stamp images. These images are stored on your device as part of your collection. PhilaTally never uploads them. You are only asked for permission when you choose to add a stamp or save an image.

Stamp lookups (Search History)

If you choose to look up a stamp online, PhilaTally sends your search text directly to the public Wikidata/Wikipedia services to return reference information. This is always initiated by you, one stamp at a time. The search text is not linked to your identity, and is used only to provide the reference result you requested. Those services have their own privacy policies.

In-app purchase

PhilaTally is free for up to 100 stamps and offers one optional in-app purchase that removes the limit. The purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store. PhilaTally never sees your payment details; it only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the purchase has been made, and remembers the answer on your device. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us or to anyone else.

Copies you make

PhilaTally has no server. A copy of your data leaves the app only when you make one:

  • Backups and exports: a backup file holds your whole collection: stamps, photos, checklists, wants, what you paid, valuations and provenance documents. A CSV export holds your stamp details. You choose where each file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Mail and so on). After that, the file is governed by the place you sent it to.
  • Shared checklists: a shared checklist file holds the checklist, its item pictures, the notes you marked as shared, and the name you chose to share it under. It never holds your stamps, what you own, prices, or your private notes.
  • Sharing a stamp or image: when you share a stamp or save its image, the photo and the text you see go to the app you pick.
  • Library for StampAlbum: PhilaTally keeps a second copy of your stamps, photos, checklists and wants in a "Philatally Library" folder, including what you paid, valuations and provenance notes, for the StampAlbum companion program to read. This is on from the first launch, and the folder is on your device, where the Files app shows it. If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive, that service stores and syncs the copy under its own privacy policy. You can switch this off in Tools › Settings › Library for StampAlbum.

Your data, your control

  • Deletion: you can delete any stamp or note in the app. Deleting the app from your device removes the data PhilaTally keeps inside the app.
  • Copies stay where you put them: deleting a stamp, or the app, does not delete backups, exports, shared files, or a Library for StampAlbum folder kept in the Files app or a synced folder. Delete those in Files or in the service that holds them.

Children

PhilaTally is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else.
